## Detailed valuation logic
The calculator now separates:
1. Exact model
2. Colour / finish
3. Storage or connectivity
4. Mac unified memory where applicable
5. Base condition
6. Detailed inspection adjustments

The inspection checklist includes:
- Activation Lock / Find My
- MDM / Remote Management
- IMEI eligibility
- Battery health
- Screen/display damage
- Back glass
- Housing/frame
- Face ID / Touch ID
- Cameras
- Charging
- Audio
- Buttons
- Parts & Service History
- Non-genuine / Unknown parts
- Logic board replacement
- Liquid/corrosion
- Third-party engraving
- Box/accessories

Activation Lock, MDM and blocked/lost/stolen IMEI are treated as eligibility blockers. The percentage deductions are NOT Apple's official rates; they are editable AppleTrade.ae starter buying-policy assumptions intended to be tuned to your actual UAE repair costs and resale margins.

## Research basis
Apple's UAE Trade In pages state that estimated values depend on the device, model and condition and can change when physical inspection finds a different condition. Apple also says a Specialist may revise the value after inspection.

Apple Support documents Parts & Service History and identifies statuses such as Genuine, Used, Unknown and Unverified. Apple specifically notes that an Unknown battery part might affect trade-in value.

## Advanced valuation rules

The calculator now applies separate adjustment factors for:
- overall grade
- battery health
- screen condition
- body/frame/back condition
- functionality
- parts/service history
- Activation Lock / MDM / carrier lock
- UAE/GCC vs international/region-specific version
- box/accessories
- 5% Trade-In bonus

These are AppleTrade.ae internal buying rules and should be calibrated against your real purchase/resale margins. They are not official Apple prices.

Research considerations used:
- Apple says final trade-in value depends on model, manufacturer and condition and can change after inspection.
- Apple advises checking Parts & Service History, Activation Lock and battery health before a sale/trade.
- Apple says a device with an Activation Lock must have it removed before trade-in.
- UAE market examples show battery health and functional/physical condition are material to buyback eligibility and price.
